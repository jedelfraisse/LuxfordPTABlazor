# Digital Dragon Intro — Storyboard (With HTML/JS Billboard + Dragon Video Placement)

## Screen Layout Overview
- **Right side:** Large HTML/JavaScript billboard (runs in the browser on the MainBoard)
- **Left side:** Video window where the animated dragon appears
- **Far left (off‑screen):** Physical stage direction the dragon will "fly toward"

The billboard and dragon video are separate elements, allowing:
- Live sponsor rotation via JavaScript
- Dragon animation to play independently
- Smooth transitions without re-rendering the whole screen

---

## Scene 1 — Idle Loop (Dragon + Sponsor Billboard)
**Duration:** Continuous loop until director cue  
**Layout:**  
- Dragon video on the **left**  
- HTML/JS billboard on the **right**

**Billboard behavior (HTML/JS):**
- Cycles through sponsor logos every 5–7 seconds
- Smooth fade or slide transitions
- Optional subtle background animation

**Dragon behavior (video loop):**
- Dragon stands or sits beside the billboard
- Presses a button, taps a touchscreen, or pulls a lever
- Each press triggers the billboard to change sponsors
- Idle animations:
  - Tail flick
  - Wing twitch
  - Slow blink
  - Small puff of smoke
  - Occasional glance at the audience

**Loop repeats seamlessly.**

---

## Scene 2 — Director Cue: “It’s Time!” Moment
**Trigger:** Director presses “Start Show”  
**Duration:** 3–5 seconds  
**Layout unchanged:** Dragon left, billboard right

**Actions:**
- Theater chimes play (ding‑ding‑ding)
- Dragon taps the button expecting another sponsor
- Billboard switches to a “Talent Show Starting” graphic
- Dragon looks confused
- Dragon checks its wrist like it has a watch
- Eyes widen in surprise
- Dragon jumps up slightly
- A magical shimmer or glitch ripple runs across its body

---

## Scene 3 — Dragon Turns Toward Audience
**Duration:** 1–2 seconds  
**Layout:** Dragon still on left, billboard dims on right

**Actions:**
- Dragon rotates toward camera/audience
- Straightens posture
- Billboard fades to a subtle glow or darkened state

---

## Scene 4 — Dragon Delivers Intro Line
**Duration:** 6–10 seconds  
**Layout:** Dragon left, billboard right (dimmed)

**Dialogue:**

> “Greetings, Luxford dragons and honored guests.  
> I am your digital master of ceremonies.  
> Please welcome your real hosts for tonight’s Talent Show, **[Name]** and **[Name]**.  
> I’ll be in the background helping each act take the stage.”

**Animation notes:**
- Mouth syncs to speech
- Occasional head tilt or blink
- Friendly, confident tone

---

## Scene 5 — Dragon Exit Animation
**Duration:** 3–4 seconds  
**Layout:** Dragon flies off toward the **left**, where the real stage is

**Actions:**
- Dragon turns toward stage-left direction
- Wings spread
- Takes a hop into the air
- Flies off-screen toward the stage
- Small sparkle trail or puff of smoke

**Billboard behavior:**
- Brightens back up
- Switches to “Act 1” or “Welcome Hosts” graphic

---

## Scene 6 — Transition to Human Hosts
**Duration:** 2–3 seconds

- Billboard displays “Act 1” or host intro
- Stage lights rise
- Human hosts walk onstage
- Dragon video window fades out or becomes a decorative frame
